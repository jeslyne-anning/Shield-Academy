EARLY YEARS

Our Early Years program is designed to foster a love of learning, creativity, and exploration. Our curriculum is play-based, child-centered, and tailored to meet the unique needs of each child.

Learning Areas: - Language and Literacy
- Mathematics
- Science and Technology
- Social Studies
- Arts and Music
- Physical Education


Key Objectives: - Develop social and emotional skills
- Encourage curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking
- Foster a love of reading, writing, and learning


Teaching Approaches: - Play-Based Learning
- Child-Centered
- Project-Based Learning
