PRINCIPAL'S ADDRESS

Education has always been the most important way of serving others, and it is the most effective vehicle for change to a better world, regardless of whether it is in our country or abroad, as the solution to every problem in human life ultimately depends on the initiatives and capacities of human beings themselves.”
Having been inspired by this brilliant concept, Shield Academy has since 1999 become a school that serves all people, regardless of race, colour or faith, in this beautiful country.
Throughout these years, we have been taught that learning is a duty for all humans. By fulfilling it, we attain the rank of true humanity and become a beneficial element of society.
We see an approach to education that is at peace with science and technology, as it warmly embraces the cultures and virtues of society at large. Hence, in this school, students of all cultures are taught universal values, ethics, and modern sciences. Our teachers do their best to prepare the students for the future by remembering, “The best way to educate people is to show real concern for every individual, not forgetting that each individual is a different world. Apart from education, the school also stresses social values and good conduct; we praise our students’ discipline, courtesy and seriousness and the high quality of education we offer in a modern, state-of-the-art environment.
I fervently hope that the Shield, which we continue to weld will take form not only to academically intelligent students but also to scholars who are resilient and versatile in every aspect of life.
~WARREN MORGAN,
PRINCIPAL
