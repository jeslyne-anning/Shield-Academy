CURRICULUM OVERVIEW


At Shield Academy , we offer a well-rounded and comprehensive curriculum designed to foster academic excellence, creativity, and critical thinking. Our curriculum is tailored to meet the diverse needs of our students, preparing them for success in an ever-changing world.

Key Features:
- Interdisciplinary Learning: Our curriculum integrates multiple subjects to promote a deeper understanding of complex concepts and issues.

- STEM Education: We offer a robust STEM program, including computer science, coding, and robotics, to equip students with essential skills for the future.

- Arts and Humanities: Our curriculum includes a rich arts program, encompassing music, visual arts, drama, and languages, to foster creativity and cultural appreciation.

-Physical Education and Wellness: We prioritize physical education, health, and wellness, recognizing the importance of a balanced lifestyle.

Academic Subjects: - English Language and Literature
- Mathematics
- Science (Biology, Chemistry, Physics)
- Social Studies (History, Geography, Civics)
- Foreign Languages (Spanish, French)
- Computer Science and Coding
- Arts and Music
- Physical Education

Electives and Extracurricular Activities: - Robotics and Engineering
- Debate and Public Speaking
- Sports Teams (Soccer, Basketball, Volleyball)
- Music and Drama Clubs

Assessment and Evaluation: - Regular quizzes and tests
- Project-based assessments
- Standardized tests (as required)
- Continuous feedback and progress monitoring

We are committed to providing a supportive and inclusive learning environment that encourages our students to grow, explore, and achieve
their full potential.
