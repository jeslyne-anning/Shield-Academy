HISTORY

Shield Academy is a coeducational international school located in Lagos , Nigeria. The school was established in 1999 to provide day and boarding to children living in Lagos and children of foreign-based families and Nigerians living outside Nigeria.

Our school emphasizes creative thinking , critical reasoning and effective communication as it encourages behaviour that is ethical , responsible and respectful to the rights of others.
