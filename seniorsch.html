SENIOR SCHOOL

Our Senior School program provides a rigorous and challenging education, preparing students for success in an ever-changing world.

Core Subjects: - English Language and Literature
- Mathematics
- Science (Biology, Chemistry, Physics)
- Social Studies (History, Geography, Economics)

Elective Subjects: - Foreign Languages (Spanish, French, Mandarin)
- Arts and Music (Visual Arts, Music, Drama)
- Computer Science and Coding
- Business and Economics

Key Features: - Project-Based Learning
- Collaborative Learning
- Technology Integration
- Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving
- Leadership and Teamwork

Assessment and Evaluation: - Regular quizzes, tests, and exams
- Project-based assessments and presentations
- Continuous feedback and progress monitoring

We offer a range of programs and courses to support the diverse needs and interests of our students.

For more information, please contact our admissions office or visit our website.
